What cookies and local storage are

Cookies and local storage are small pieces of data a website saves in your browser. They let a site remember things between visits, such as your preferences.

agentdna.io doesn’t set advertising or tracking cookies. It uses your browser’s local storage for a few preferences, listed below.

What we store

Everything below stays in your browser. None of it is sent to us.

Third parties

The site’s typefaces load from Google Fonts. Your browser connects to Google’s servers to fetch them, which shares your IP address with Google; see Google’s privacy policy. Links to our documentation, GitHub, LinkedIn and X take you to sites with their own cookie policies.

Managing storage in your browser

You can also clear or block cookies and site data in your browser’s settings. Blocking strictly necessary storage may stop parts of the site from working as intended.
